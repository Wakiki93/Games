Greybox concept prototype ·  · single-player · played with a controller · not a feel test

    
This prototype is played with a game controller (Xbox layout) on a computer. Open it in Chrome or Edge on a PC to play.

    
Hold a real neighborhood against escalating rounds. Every building is cover — and the horde (Breachers) or your own breach charges can bring it down. Each round, capture and hold the objective sector for bonus Salvage.

    
Controller: none detected. Connect one, then press any button on your controller.

    
Controllers may be blocked in this viewer. To use a controller, open the standalone index.html file in Chrome or Edge.
